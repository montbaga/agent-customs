Protect your assistant in one line

For any site made with Cognizant's agentify-website kit. No server, no build step, and the runtime stays unchanged.

  1. 1

    Copy the line. It loads the whole guard as one file, and each visitor's browser checks its fingerprint before running it.

  2. 2

    Paste it above your page's other scripts, in the page that holds your assistant.

  3. 3

    That's it. Every partner your assistant calls is checked before it runs. Denied partners never run, changed ones wait for review, and visitors see the stamp in the chat.

      Official baselines

        Approved versions

        Approving a network means you trust this exact version of it. Agent Customs keeps its fingerprint in this browser only. If the site later changes its agents or its manifest, even by one line, the guard holds the new version for your review instead of running it. To approve, inspect a network on the Report tab and press Approve this version; a denied network cannot be approved. The officer can also approve or forget one for you, but only after you confirm. Forget removes an approval.

                Five checks, one stamp

                Every network takes the same route. Rules decide the verdict and the AI explains it. If you turn on the AI double-check, your AI can only make a verdict stricter.

                1. Network URL
                2. Agent Customs
                3. Papers · Contraband · Itinerary · Privacy · Identity
                4. Verdict
                • Rules decide, the model explains. The AI reads text written by strangers, so it can never clear a network. The optional AI double-check can only hold one back.
                • Nothing leaves your browser. No server, no cookies, no key sent. Approvals stay on this device. The AI double-check, if you turn it on, sends a network's public text to your own AI provider.
                • Fails closed. If a partner cannot be inspected, it does not run.
